Homestead Exemption

A homestead exemption lowers the taxable value of a home that the owner lives in as their main residence. The exemption applies to the property you occupy on January 1 of the tax year.

How to apply

File an application with the assessor's office, in person or by post. Include proof that you live at the property, such as a driver's licence showing that address. Applications by email are not accepted.
